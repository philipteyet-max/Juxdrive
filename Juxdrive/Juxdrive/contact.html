FAQ

Common
Questions

Can't find the answer you need? Send us a message above and we'll respond within 2 hours.

How do I book a driver?
+
You can book directly through our website using the "Book a Driver" button, call us at +(233) 57 400 2145, or send an email to info@juxdrive.org. A team member will confirm your booking within 30 minutes.
Can the driver use my own car?
+
Yes — this is one of our most popular services. Our professional chauffeurs can drive your personal vehicle. Simply provide the car details at booking and we'll match you with an appropriately licensed driver.
How much does a booking cost?
+
Pricing starts at GH₵80 for standard bookings and varies based on service type, distance, duration, and vehicle class. Use our fare calculator on the homepage for an instant estimate, or contact us directly for a custom quote.
Do you operate outside of Accra?
+
Yes. We cover Greater Accra Region and offer overnight and inter-city trips across Ghana, including Cape Coast, Kumasi, Takoradi, and other major cities. Contact us for pricing on long-distance routes.
How will I know where my driver is?
+
On the day of your ride, you'll receive real-time SMS and email updates on your driver's current location throughout the journey. You'll also receive the driver's contact number when your booking is confirmed.
What is your cancellation policy?
+
Cancellations made more than 24 hours before the booking are fully refunded. Cancellations within 24 hours may incur a partial charge. Please contact us as early as possible if your plans change.
