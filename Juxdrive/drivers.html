Our Standards

How We Vet
Every Driver

We don't just hire drivers — we build a team of trusted professionals. Every JuxDrive chauffeur passes a rigorous multi-step vetting process before their first ride.

1
Application & Interview

Every candidate submits a formal application and undergoes an in-person interview with our operations team to assess professionalism and attitude.

2
Licence & Document Verification

Valid DVLA driving licence, national ID, and any applicable certifications are cross-verified with relevant authorities.

3
Background & Criminal Check

A full criminal background screening is conducted to ensure every driver has a clean record before joining the JuxDrive team.

4
Driving Assessment & Training

A practical road test followed by training in customer service, safety protocols, and the JuxDrive standard of conduct.
